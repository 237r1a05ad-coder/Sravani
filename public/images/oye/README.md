# Photos for Scene 5 (The Six of Us)

Place genuine photographs of the gang and Sravani into this folder:

- `group-01.jpg`, `group-02.jpg` (Group memories)
- `school-01.jpg` (School days)
- `movie-night-01.jpg` (Bhargavi's house movie nights)
- `sravani-01.jpg` (Individual photos of Sravani / Oye)

Once added, register them in `src/data/galleryConfig.js`.
Images will automatically display in Scene 5 with their natural aspect ratios and lightbox zoom.
